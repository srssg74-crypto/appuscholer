❓ Help & Troubleshooting

Most problems have a quick fix below. Check here first — it's faster than waiting on an email.

🔑 My access code won't work

Paste the whole code, including the dot in the middle, with no extra spaces or line breaks — copying from some apps adds a stray character at the start or end. Codes also expire; if the date has passed, you'll need a fresh one from whoever gave you Appuscholar.

🔌 My AI key stopped working, or I lost it

Open ⚙ AI Provider / Key at the top right, pick your provider again, and follow the link to generate a new one — it's free and takes under a minute. Your key lives only in this browser; nothing to recover from a server.

💾 My work disappeared after closing the tab or clearing my browser

Everything is saved only in this browser, on this computer — never on a server. Clearing site data, switching browsers, or moving to a new computer wipes it with no way back. Use ⬇ Download in the document toolbar regularly to keep a copy outside the browser.

🖥 The page looks broken, or won't load properly

Try a hard refresh — Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac — or open it in Chrome or Edge if you're on something else. Both render this app best.

हि Something stayed in English after switching to Hindi

A few things stay in English on purpose — statistical test names, citation style names (APA, MLA), and the external AI-provider sign-up pages — since those are the exact terms you'll meet everywhere else too. Anything other than that is a genuine bug worth reporting below.
