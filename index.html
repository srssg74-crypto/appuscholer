💜 Welcome to Appupsychoscholar

A writing assistant for psychology students. Everything you write stays on this computer.

  1. Click ⚙ AI Provider / Key at the top right.
  2. Pick Google Gemini or Groq — both have a free tier.
  3. Follow the link, create a key, paste it in, press Save Key.
  4. Start writing. Press Tab to accept a suggestion.

Appupsychoscholar drafts and checks — it does not do your thinking. Verify every citation, and check your institution's policy on AI assistance before you submit.

📊 Statistics Lab

Paste data from Excel, SPSS, Google Sheets or a CSV file — first row = column names. Everything is computed on this computer; your data is never sent anywhere unless you tap "Explain in plain language" below, and even then only the computed numbers are sent, not your raw data.

📐 These formulas cover the common cases correctly (Student's independent t, average-rank non-parametric tests, standard ANOVA/chi-square). For an unusual design, or before a dissertation defense, cross-check against SPSS or JASP.
